Privacy policy

Family Command Center is a private household dashboard. It is used only by the members of one family.

What it accesses

When a family member connects their Google account, the app reads their calendar events, reads their Gmail messages, and reads and writes files in Google Drive for the family photo frame. It requests read access wherever it can, and uses these only to show the family's own schedule, notices and photos on the family's own screens.

Where the data goes

The data is stored in the family's own private database and on the family's own home computer. It is never sold, shared with other people, used for advertising, or used to train any model. Google account sign-in tokens are stored encrypted for the app's own use.

Control

A family member can disconnect their Google account in the app at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the app stops reading that account.
